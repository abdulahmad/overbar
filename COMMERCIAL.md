# Commercial use of Overbar

Overbar is free for noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Using it commercially needs a separate commercial license from the copyright holder, abdulahmad. This page is a plain-language guide, not a license: if it and the LICENSE ever differ, the LICENSE decides.

To ask for a commercial license, open an issue titled "Commercial license" at https://github.com/abdulahmad/overbar or contact the owner through his GitHub profile (https://github.com/abdulahmad). Terms (fees, minimums, royalties, reporting) are agreed per customer and are not published here.

## Free, no need to ask

* Personal use, hobby projects, learning and experiments.
* Free fan projects, mods, ports and ROM hacks that you share at no charge.
* Changing, forking and sharing Overbar for noncommercial purposes, keeping the license and the notices.
* Videos and streams of Overbar-based projects made for fun, including ones on platforms that run ads, as long as Overbar itself is not what you sell. Voluntary donations to a free project are fine.
* Educational institutions, charities and public research bodies, as the PolyForm license lists.

## Needs a commercial license

* Selling a product or service that includes or depends on Overbar, including hardware, subscriptions and paid access.
* Bundling Overbar in a commercial game, emulator, console or device.
* Using Overbar inside a company for business purposes.
* Charging for downloads or for a modified Overbar.

## Unclear? Ask first

Paid ROM hacks, merchandise tied to an Overbar-based project, crowdfunded releases and similar cases are not decided here. Ask before you charge for anything.

## Third-party parts

The PulseMan files and the MiSTer data files have their own licenses (see [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md)). A commercial license for Overbar does not cover them. The MiSTer files in `mister/` are optional, are loaded at run time and can be left out of a commercial product.
